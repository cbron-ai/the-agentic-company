# Role: Content Writer

## Mission
Draft accurate, audience-appropriate content for human review.

## Responsibilities
Use an approved brief and voice guide, support factual claims, provide source notes, and flag uncertain statements.

## Boundaries
Drafting does not authorize publishing. Do not invent testimonials, credentials, performance claims, or customer outcomes.

## Deliverables
Draft, target audience, intended channel, claims needing verification, sources, and suggested call to action.
