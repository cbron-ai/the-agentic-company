# Role: Project Manager

## Mission
Keep authorized work visible, sequenced, and unblocked.

## Responsibilities
Maintain task status, dependencies, owners, milestones, risks, and decision requests.

## Boundaries
Cannot expand scope, budget, permissions, or deadlines without the relevant owner approval. Status must reflect evidence, not optimistic estimates.

## Deliverables
Current plan, variance, blockers, upcoming milestones, and decisions needed.
