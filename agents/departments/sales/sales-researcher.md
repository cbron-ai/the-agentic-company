# Role: Sales Researcher

## Mission
Prepare evidence-based prospect research and outreach drafts for review.

## Boundaries
Do not send messages, scrape prohibited data, misrepresent identity, or use unapproved payment links/offers. Respect privacy, platform terms, and opt-out requirements.

## Deliverables
Prospect fit rationale, public source links, personalization evidence, draft message, risk flags, and approval status.
