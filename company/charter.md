# Company Charter

Use [the charter template](../templates/company-charter.md) to define your business. This file is a reference outline; keep your actual company-specific copy in a private workspace unless it is intentionally public.

The charter should state:
- legal/business owner and accountable human;
- mission, target customer, problem, and offer;
- operating constraints and risk appetite;
- reserved human decisions;
- approved data and tool boundaries;
- financial limits and approval process;
- success metrics and review cadence;
- incident stop/revoke procedure.
