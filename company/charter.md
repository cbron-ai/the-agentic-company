# Company Charter

This is a reference outline. Keep company-specific values in a private workspace unless intentionally public. Start from [the editable template](../templates/company-charter.md).

The charter must identify the Human Board, Muse as AI CEO, Claude as Shadow of the Board, specialist roles, mission, target customer, approved projects, reserved decisions, delegated authority, tool/data boundaries, budgets, success metrics, reporting cadence, approval channel, evidence location, and tested stop/revoke procedure.

The charter is the operating contract. No role prompt or agent-generated plan may silently override it.
