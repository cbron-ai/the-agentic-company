# Operating Principles

1. Customer value before agent activity.
2. Human accountability is never delegated away.
3. Small, explicit scopes beat broad vague mandates.
4. Least privilege is the default.
5. Approval is explicit, scoped, and time-bounded.
6. Evidence and tests are part of the deliverable.
7. Costs include subscriptions and human time.
8. Reversibility and recovery are designed before execution.
9. Agent roles are added only for recurring work.
10. Stop or simplify workflows that do not demonstrate value.
