# Financial Controls

AI operations can create costs through model usage, subscriptions, cloud infrastructure, APIs, advertising, refunds, and human review.

## Budget policy

For each project define:
- owner and business purpose;
- approved vendors and spend categories;
- monthly and per-run limits;
- alert thresholds;
- who can authorize exceptions;
- treatment of recurring subscriptions;
- cancellation and renewal review dates.

## Track total cost

Include direct API/model charges, allocated subscriptions, infrastructure, payment fees, refunds, and human time. If subscription usage is not measurable, label it as an estimate rather than zero.

## Business scorecard

Track customer outcomes, revenue, gross margin, retention, acquisition cost, defect/rework rate, time-to-delivery, founder hours, and total operating cost. Agent task count and token count are operational diagnostics, not business success metrics.

Never allow an agent to approve its own budget increase or execute an unbounded purchase.
