# Incident Management

An incident is any event that causes or could cause unauthorized access, data loss, harmful external communication, financial loss, production disruption, or violation of policy.

## Immediate response

1. Stop the affected workflow and prevent further actions.
2. Revoke or disable relevant credentials/tokens where appropriate.
3. Preserve logs and evidence without spreading secrets or personal data.
4. Notify the accountable human owner.
5. Assess impact, affected systems, customers, and legal/reporting obligations.
6. Recover from a known-good state.
7. Record root causes and corrective actions.
8. Test that the fix prevents recurrence.

Do not let the agent that caused an incident be the sole investigator or approver of remediation.
