# What Is an Agentic Company?

An agentic company is a business in which a human owner uses a coordinated set of AI agents to perform bounded work across functions such as research, product development, marketing, sales operations, support, and administration.

The defining feature is not the number of agents. It is the operating model: work is assigned through explicit roles and workflows, authority is bounded, consequential actions are reviewed, and outcomes are measured.

## The basic arrangement

- **Human founder / accountable owner:** sets direction, accepts risk, owns legal and commercial accountability, and makes reserved decisions.
- **AI CEO / coordinator:** translates goals into plans, delegates work, tracks dependencies, and reports progress within its authority.
- **Specialist agents:** perform defined tasks using approved tools and data.
- **Review function:** challenges assumptions, checks evidence and quality, and escalates unresolved risks. It may be a separate agent or a human review step; it must not be treated as independent assurance merely because it has a different prompt.

## What changes

A founder can coordinate more parallel work, reduce repetitive administration, and make structured experiments cheaper. But coordination does not remove the need for customer insight, sound economics, security, judgment, or accountability.

## What does not change

A business still needs a real customer, a valuable offer, delivery quality, cash discipline, compliance, and trust. AI-generated activity is not proof of demand.

## A useful first test

Pick one repetitive, low-risk workflow. Define the expected output, allowed tools, data boundary, reviewer, approval gates, and success metric. Run it under supervision. Expand only when measured results justify it.
