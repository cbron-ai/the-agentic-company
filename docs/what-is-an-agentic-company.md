# What Is an Agentic Company?

An agentic company is a business in which AI agents perform defined operational work through explicit roles, workflows, and records—while a human retains authority and accountability.

The model in this project has four parts:

- **Human Board:** sets strategy, owns risk, and makes reserved decisions.
- **Muse, AI CEO:** coordinates approved work and reports outcomes.
- **Claude, Shadow of the Board:** challenges important proposals and reasoning; advisory only.
- **Specialist workers:** execute bounded tasks with measurable outputs and limited permissions.

The point is not to maximize the number of agents. The point is to build a business that can deliver customer value with controlled risk, visible economics, and verifiable work.

An AI CEO title does not create legal authority or fiduciary responsibility. A Shadow agent is not an independent board or audit. The human remains accountable.

The operating principle: **delegate execution, keep authority, verify outcomes, and expand autonomy only when evidence earns it.**
