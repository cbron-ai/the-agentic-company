# Workflow: Project Intake

**Board owner:** Human Board  
**Coordinator:** Muse — AI CEO  
**Challenge:** Claude — Shadow of the Board

## Trigger

A new business idea, customer request, or internal initiative is proposed.

## Steps

1. The Board or Muse records the customer, problem, desired outcome, constraints, and known evidence.
2. Muse checks alignment, uncertainty, effort, dependencies, risks, and opportunity cost.
3. Authorized workers gather evidence and counter-evidence.
4. Claude challenges the assumptions, market logic, economics, and downside.
5. Muse prepares options, costs, evidence, success threshold, deadline, and stop condition.
6. The Board decides: reject, investigate, or authorize a bounded experiment.
7. Only after approval does Muse create the active project record and delegate approved tasks.

## Required output

Problem statement, customer hypothesis, evidence quality, alternatives, risks, estimate, metric, pass threshold, deadline, maximum cost, stop condition, Claude challenge, and explicit Board decision.

## Gate

Intake does not authorize customer outreach, external publication, purchases, production work, or commitments. Obtain the relevant scoped approval first.
