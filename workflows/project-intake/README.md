# Workflow: Project Intake

**Owner:** Human founder  
**Coordinator:** AI CEO  
**Risk:** Low until execution or external commitments are authorized

## Trigger
A new business idea, customer request, or internal initiative is proposed.

## Steps
1. Founder records the problem, target customer, desired outcome, constraints, and known evidence.
2. AI CEO checks alignment, uncertainty, likely effort, dependencies, and risks.
3. Market researcher gathers approved evidence and counter-evidence.
4. Reviewer challenges assumptions and identifies missing validation.
5. AI CEO presents options, costs, and a recommendation.
6. Founder decides whether to reject, investigate, or authorize a bounded experiment.
7. If authorized, create a project brief and budget.

## Required output
Problem statement, customer hypothesis, evidence, alternatives, risks, estimate, success metric, stop condition, and explicit founder decision.

## Gate
No agent may turn intake into customer outreach, spend, publication, or production work without the relevant authorization.
