# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, constructive community.

Expected behavior:
- Be respectful and assume good intent without avoiding hard technical criticism.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Welcome different experience levels and perspectives.
- Be clear about uncertainty, trade-offs, and limitations.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, and publishing private information without consent.

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions that violate this policy. For safety or conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers through GitHub.
