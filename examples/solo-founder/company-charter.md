# Example Company Charter — Illustrative Only

- Business: early-stage software product experiment
- Accountable owner: human founder
- Customer hypothesis: a narrowly defined small-business segment with a repeated workflow problem
- Offer: prototype to test willingness to use/pay
- Budget: founder-defined; no agent may initiate spend
- Data: synthetic and public data only during prototype
- External communication: founder approval required
- Production: no production deployment in this experiment
- Success: qualified customer conversations, observed problem frequency, prototype usage, willingness-to-pay evidence, and realistic unit economics
- Stop condition: no credible problem evidence after the agreed discovery period, or costs exceed the approved cap
