# Example Charter (Illustrative)

- Human Board: one accountable human.
- AI CEO: Muse.
- Shadow of the Board: Claude (advisory only).
- Initial scope: one validated customer problem and one low-risk workflow.
- Reserved: strategy, project start/stop, new spend, pricing, legal commitments, permissions, external publication, production deployment, money movement, destructive actions.
- Delegated: planning and task coordination inside the approved project.
- Budget: no agent-authorized new spend until the Board records a limit.
- Stop: pause on missing approval, scope drift, security concern, budget cap, or unverified external side effect.
